Premium seafood and global gourmet foods, delivered with care

{{legal_name}} is a premium food sourcing and distribution company based in New Delhi, specialising in high-quality seafood and international gourmet products for hotels, restaurants, cafés, caterers and retail partners.

What we do

We source premium frozen seafood, Japanese and Asian specialty ingredients, imported cheese and quality chicken and lamb, store them in our own cold storage, and supply them to professional kitchens and food businesses.

Our customers are chefs, purchase managers and owners who need specialty ingredients to arrive as expected — so their menus stay consistent.

How we work

  • Own cold storage. Products are held at maintained temperatures in our own facilities.
  • Shelf life checked. Every order is checked before dispatch so expired stock never reaches a customer.
  • Segregated storage. Vegetarian and non-vegetarian products are stored separately.
  • Customer care. A dedicated team supports orders, availability and after-sales questions.
  • Pan-India supply. Based in Delhi NCR, supplying customers across India.
