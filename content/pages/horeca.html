Questions from purchase teams

Do you supply outside Delhi NCR?

Yes. We are based in New Delhi and supply customers across India. Delivery method and timelines depend on your location and order — our team will confirm them with your quotation.

Are you FSSAI licensed and GST registered?

Yes. We hold FSSAI Central Licence No. 13325999000401 and GSTIN 07AALCD3845N1Z4. Copies can be shared for vendor onboarding on request.

Do you publish prices?

No. Prices depend on product, pack size, volume and shipment, so we quote per account. Request a quote and we will respond with current pricing.

How do you store products before dispatch?

Products are held in our own cold storage facilities with maintained temperatures. Vegetarian and non-vegetarian products are stored separately, and every order is checked for shelf life before dispatch.

Can I order seafood, Japanese pantry and cheese together?

Yes — consolidating categories into a single order is one of the main reasons kitchens work with us.
